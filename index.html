A practical checklist

Research public information with care.

Check the source, date and evidence behind information before forming an opinion.

01 / SOURCE

Check the original source

Identify who published the information and what evidence is included.

02 / CONTEXT

Compare different accounts

Distinguish verifiable details from opinions and individual experiences.

03 / DATE

Check publication dates

Confirm that programme details and policies are current.

04 / TERMS

Read terms carefully

Review pricing, access conditions and refund policies before purchasing.
