A Practical Checklist

How to Research a Public Figure or Programme

Before forming an opinion about an author, company or educational programme, use a few simple checks to understand what the available information actually supports.

01. Check the Original Source Determine who published the information and what evidence it provides.
02. Compare Different Accounts Distinguish verifiable details from individual opinions and experiences.
03. Check Publication Dates Confirm that details remain relevant and current.
04. Read Terms Carefully Review pricing, access conditions and refund policies before purchasing a course.
